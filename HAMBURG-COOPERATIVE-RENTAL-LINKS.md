# Hamburg cooperative rental links

The [Hamburger Wohnungsbaugenossenschaften directory](https://wohnungsbaugenossenschaften-hh.de/genossenschaften/) displays 29 cooperative profiles, although its introduction says there are 30 members. This list covers the 29 displayed profiles.

**In extension** refers to the extension's default portal configuration, not any personal changes made in a saved dashboard. Some cooperatives are represented by an Immomio landlord page or an Immosolve portal rather than the website linked below. Offer pages can require registration or consent and do not guarantee that a vacancy is currently available.
